==Workshops==
These tutorials present three levels of workshop where you can build all of the special blocks and items in Create, as well as wide variety of contraptions.  

* [[Starter Workshop]]: The starter workshop can be built entirely with materials sourced from the overworld. Some of the key things it will allow you to do are automatic harvesting and mining.
* [[Brass Workshop]]: This intermediate level workshop requires resources from the Nether. Some of the key capabilities it will open up are automatic item sorting, advanced redstone circuits, ore processors, and elevators.
* [[Precision Workshop]]: This advanced workshop allows you to build anything in the Create Mod. Some of the key capabilities it opens up are trains, logistics networks, and rotation speed controllers.
