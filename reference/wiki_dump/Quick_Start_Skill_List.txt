|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
'''1. Make Andesite Alloy'''
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|Andesite Alloy is a fundamental ingredient for nearly every item in Create. You will need '''a lot''' of it.
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
1. Mine iron ore and andesite blocks<br>
2. Use JEI to find the recipe for Andesite Alloy<br>
3. Make a full stack
|-
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
'''2. Ride a spinning turntable'''
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|Rotational power is needed to run machines. Learn to generate power and control its speed and stress capacity.
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
1. Make a stack of [[Shaft|Shafts]]. Use JEI for the recipe.<br>
2. Make a half stack of [[Cogwheel|Cogwheels]].<br>
3. Make a half dozen [[Cogwheel|Large Cogwheels]].<br>
4. Make two [[Gearbox|Gearboxes]]. Consult the [[Build Order]] page and trace the dependencies for a gear box.<br>
5. Make and install a [[Water Wheel]].<br>
6. Explore the use of shafts, cogwheels, and gearboxes to increase/decrease rotation speed and change the direction of rotation.<br>
7. Build a [[Turntable]]. Place it on a rotating vertical shaft, then stand on it.
|-
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
'''3. Make a Slimeball'''
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|Learn the use of a mill and some of the recipes it provides.
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
1. Build a [[Millstone]] and connect it to your rotational power source. Run it at the fastest speed you can.<br>
2. Create [[Dough]] by milling wheat into flour and combining with a bucket of water on a crafting table.<br>
3. Combine dough with {{MC|Lime Dye}} on a crafting table.
|-
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
'''4. Cut down a tree with a [[Mechanical Saw]]'''
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|Build your first Create contraption! Build your starter workshop, learn about mechanical pistons, mechanical presses and gearshifts. See how the mechanical saw cuts down the whole tree at once.
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
1. Build a [[Starter Workshop]]<br>
2. Build eight [[Piston Extension Pole|Piston Extension Poles]] <br>
3. Build a [[Mechanical Piston]]<br>
4. Create 3 [[Iron Sheet|Iron Sheets]] on a [[Mechanical Press]]<br>
5. Build a [[Mechanical Saw]]<br>
6. Build a [[Gearshift]]<br>
7. Place the mechanical piston on the ground horizontally with two extra extension poles.<br>
8. Attach the gearshift to the mechanical piston and to a rotational power source.<br>
9. Attach a {{Minecraft|Lever}} to the gearshift. This will allow you to move the piston in and out.<br>
10. Coat the piston with slime from a slime ball, then attach the mechanical saw.<br>
11. Activate the lever until the piston fully retracts.<br>
12. Plant a tree two blocks in front of the saw.<br>
13. When the tree matures, activate the lever to push the saw into the tree.
|-
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
'''5. Mine some ore with a contraption'''
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|Create a minecart contraption. Learn about mechanical drills, cart assemblers, placement assist and the various issues influencing the design of mining contraptions. 
|style="border: 1px solid #ccc; padding: 8px; vertical-align: top; text-align: left;"|
1. Build a dozen [[Mechanical Drill|Mechanical Drills]]<br>
2. Build 15 [[Linear Chassis]]<br>
3. Build a [[Cart Assembler]]<br>
4. Build a [[Wrench]]<br>
5. Build a minecart, rail, and powered rail.<br>
6. Build 2 chests.<br>
7. Review [[Tutorials/Mining Contraptions]] and build a simple 3 x 4 mining contraption with manual rails and no portable storage interface. Use [[Placement Assist]] to keep the drills aligned.<br>
8. Use the cart assembler to assemble the contraption and use the wrench to pick up the assembled contraption.<br>
9. Lay rail along a tunnel in rock. Place the contraption on powered rail and give it a push to start.<br>
